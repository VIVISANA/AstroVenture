# AstroVenture
AstroVenture is an immersive, space-themed educational experience designed to make space science engaging, approachable, and inspiring. It invites learners to explore the universe through interactive content that introduces key ideas in astronomy and space science, encourages curiosity about how the cosmos works, and turns complex topics into meaningful learning experiences.

The project also helps learners discover the people, pathways, and possibilities behind STEM careers. By connecting space exploration with real-world science, technology, engineering, and mathematics, AstroVenture encourages students to imagine themselves contributing to the future of discovery and innovation. Its learner-focused experience is intended for students, educators, and anyone curious about space who wants to learn at their own pace in an engaging digital environment.
